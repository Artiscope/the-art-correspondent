The Studio Sessions

Original interviews, photographs and short films, made in the room where the work is made.

Photograph to come. From the first session.

What a session is

Each session is a visit to one artist's studio. We sit down for an interview, photograph the space and the work in progress, and make a short film of the artist at work.

Our rule on interviews

Every conversation we publish is a real one. An interview appears here only after it has taken place and the artist has approved the transcript. We do not write imagined or reconstructed dialogue.

Where things stand

The first sessions are being recorded now. Until they are ready, you will find reported features and essays across the magazine.
