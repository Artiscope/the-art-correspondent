Exhibitions

Reports from shows, openings and installations.

Shows and installations in our reporting

  1. Konn Artiss, ANTI-FRIEZE

    Outside Frieze London, October 2025

    Five blocks of ice, each holding a miniature effigy of a famous artist, left to melt at the entrance to the fair.

  2. Beyond The Surface

    Grove Gallery, Palma, April 2025

    The opening exhibition of a new gallery space, with work by Zara Muse, Amanza Smith and Silent Mike.

  3. Silent Mike, Chromatic Shift

    Dorsett Shepherds Bush, London, opened 28 March 2025

    Fifteen works in colour hung through the hotel bar and restaurant, the first show in a gallery residency.

  4. Zara Muse at Fairmont Windsor Park

    Windsor, reported November 2024

    Fourteen portraits hung in the hotel bar and restaurant as part of a year-long partnership with Grove Gallery.

  5. Zara Muse, Winds of Change: A Journey Unfolding

    Grove Gallery, London, autumn 2024

    A solo exhibition that FAD Magazine reported was extended by two weeks because of demand.

  6. Zen Xander, Society Falling

    Grove Gallery, Fitzrovia, London, opened 26 June 2024

    A first solo exhibition of stencil and spray paint works on plywood, with animals standing in for people.

  7. Amoako Boafo, Proper Love

    Belvedere, Vienna, 2024

    More than fifty finger-painted portraits, some hung beside Egon Schiele and Gustav Klimt.

  8. El Anatsui, Behind the Red Moon

    Tate Modern, London, 10 October 2023 to 14 April 2024

    Three vast hangings of bottle caps and copper wire in the Turbine Hall, made for the Hyundai Commission.

  9. Zara Muse, Radiant Resilience

    Grove Gallery, Fitzrovia, London, September 2023

    Her first exhibition at the gallery.

  10. Clarke Reynolds at Quantus Gallery

    London, early 2023

    A solo exhibition of braille works made to be seen and touched.

  11. Njideka Akunyili Crosby, The Beautyful Ones

    National Portrait Gallery, London, 2018

    A series of portraits built from paint, collage and photo transfer.

These notes come from published reports. First-hand exhibition reviews will be added here as they are filed.
