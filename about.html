About

The people, practice and culture magazine.

The Art Correspondent explores contemporary art through the people who make, present and collect it.

Through studio reporting, interviews, cultural essays and visual storytelling, we examine the ideas, experiences and creative decisions behind today's artistic practices.

What we publish

Features and reported essays. Artist stories. Notes from the studio on materials and working methods. Writing on culture and exhibitions. And The Studio Sessions, our series of interviews, photographs and short films.

How we work

We publish an interview only when it has really taken place and the transcript has been approved by the person interviewed. Until then, a piece is a feature or a reported essay and is described as one.
