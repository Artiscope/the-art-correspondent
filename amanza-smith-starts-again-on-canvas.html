Amanza Smith Starts Again on Canvas

By The Art Correspondent. Published 19 November 2026.

Television audiences know the name already. The paintings ask to be looked at without it.

Amanza Smith came to painting with a public profile. She is known to television viewers from the property series Selling Sunset, and her gallery describes her simply as multifaceted. The question any such artist faces is whether the work holds up once the name is covered.

A debut in Los Angeles

She made her debut as an exhibiting artist at MASH Gallery in Los Angeles. The gallery's account notes that she is from Indiana and calls her no stranger to reinvention.

What the paintings are made of

The work is mixed media on canvas, often large. In The Groove, from 2024, measures 130 centimetres square and, according to the gallery's notes, brings collage into the paint, including images of Frida Kahlo. City Zen, from the same year, is larger still and is described as graffiti lifted into fine art, a reflection on life in a big city.

More recent pieces turn to machines. Gallery notes for works such as Circuit Riot describe robotic forms, circuitry and glitch-like marks set against loose, gestural paint.

Words in the paint

Text appears too. Of the painting To The Bone, the gallery reports that she wrote the word love across the surface several times, as a deliberate act of encouragement.

Some viewers will find that too direct. But it is consistent. These are open-hearted pictures, more interested in energy than in irony.

To Europe

In April 2025 her work crossed the Atlantic. Grove Gallery opened a space in Palma with a first exhibition, Beyond The Surface, showing her alongside Zara Muse and Silent Mike.

A fair test

Fame gets an artist through the door quickly, and it also means many people decide what they think before they look. The fair test is the ordinary one. Stand in front of the canvas. See whether the colour, the layering and the marks keep your attention once you stop thinking about who made them.
